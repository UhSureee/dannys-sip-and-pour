Frequently asked questions

Do you provide the alcohol?

No. Clients provide all alcoholic beverages. We will recommend what to purchase and prepare a personalized shopping list based on your menu and guest count.

What is included with each package?

Every package includes Daniel and Daniela, up to 75 guests, three hours of service, setup, breakdown, planning support, and our Speakeasy Special. Additional inclusions depend on the package you select.

What is the Speakeasy Special?

It is a secret, off-menu cocktail created for your honored guest or guests. It is our way of adding an unexpected and personal moment to every celebration.

How far do you travel?

Our standard service area is approximately 50 miles from Pomona. We may travel farther when transportation and related expenses are covered.
